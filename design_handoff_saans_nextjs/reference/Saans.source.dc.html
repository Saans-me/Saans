Is it time to reach out?

There's no wrong time.

  • Low mood or worry has lasted two weeks or more
  • Sleep, appetite or energy have changed a lot
  • Work, studies or relationships are getting harder to manage
  • Things you used to enjoy no longer feel the same
  • You'd simply like to talk to a trained person

WHO IT'S FOR

Support that meets you where you are.

Different days need different kinds of help. Start wherever feels right, and move between levels whenever you need to.

Companionship

When you feel alone

Even with people around, it's possible to feel unheard. saans is a friendly companion you can talk to whenever you need.

  • Everyday conversations by text or voice
  • A non-judgemental space to share anything
  • Optional mood check-ins and a private journal
  • Hindi, Hinglish, English and regional languages

Built to support your real-world relationships, never to replace them.

Everyday support

When things feel heavy

Stress, overthinking, pressure at work or college, relationship worries, low mood. saans helps you name it and find what helps.

  • Guided conversations to understand your feelings
  • Mood and pattern tracking over time
  • Breathing, grounding and coping exercises
  • A gentle nudge toward a professional if things persist

saans doesn't diagnose. It helps you reflect and cope, and seek help early.

Professional care

When it's been hard for a while

If low mood has lasted, or getting through the day feels hard, a conversation alone may not be enough. We help you reach human care.

  • Supportive conversations within clear safety limits
  • Verified professionals, online or in person
  • Share approved notes with your professional
  • Follow-up reminders, only with your consent

saans doesn't treat depression on its own. Qualified people do, and we help you get to them.
